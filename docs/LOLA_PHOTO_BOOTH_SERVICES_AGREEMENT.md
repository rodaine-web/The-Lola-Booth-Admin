### 2. Booking Retainer Fee and Confirmation

A **30% Non-Refundable Booking Retainer Fee** is required to initiate the booking process and reserve the requested event date, subject to completion of the booking requirements.

The booking retainer fee is credited toward the total contract amount and is not an additional fee.

The booking retainer fee is intended to compensate LOLA for reserving the requested date, allocating resources, and declining potentially conflicting bookings.

The booking is fully confirmed only when:

1. The proposal has been accepted.
2. The required booking retainer fee has been successfully paid.
3. This Agreement has been electronically signed by all required parties.
4. LOLA has issued written booking confirmation.

Until all requirements are satisfied, the booking remains pending confirmation.

The booking retainer fee is not refunded in cash when Client cancels, reschedules, or otherwise changes the booking. It is included in the 100% rebooking credit described in Sections 6 and 7. If LOLA cannot fulfill its contracted obligations, LOLA will issue a refund as described in Sections 9, 17 and 21.

### 3. Payment Schedule

Unless otherwise specified in the accepted proposal, the remaining **70% balance is due fourteen (14) calendar days before the scheduled event date**.

For bookings made fewer than fourteen (14) days before the event, full payment is required at confirmation unless LOLA approves an alternative arrangement in writing.

Client is responsible for ensuring all required payments are received by their respective due dates.

LOLA may approve alternative payment schedules for corporate clients or special events. Such arrangements must be documented in writing.

### 4. Partial Payments

### 5. Payment Reminders and Late Payment

LOLA may send payment reminders five (5) days, three (3) days, and twenty-four (24) hours before the scheduled payment due date.

### 6. Cancellation by Client

Client may request cancellation through The Client Workspace or by sending written notice to **info@thelolabooth.com**.

LOLA requests at least forty-eight (48) hours' written notice before the scheduled event.

LOLA does not issue cash refunds when Client cancels, reschedules, or otherwise changes the booking. This policy applies to all payments received, including the 30% booking retainer fee.

When LOLA finalizes a Client cancellation, LOLA will provide rebooking credit equal to 100% of payments received for the cancelled booking, including the booking retainer fee, under Section 7.

A Client cancellation results in rebooking credit, not a cash refund.

A cancellation becomes effective when LOLA confirms receipt and processes the request in accordance with this Agreement.

LOLA will provide written confirmation of the cancellation and the rebooking credit issued.

### 7. Rebooking Credits

When LOLA finalizes a Client cancellation, LOLA will issue rebooking credit equal to 100% of payments received for the cancelled booking, including the 30% booking retainer fee, as rebooking credit only. Client cancellation does not result in a cash refund.

Amounts already refunded, reversed, or charged back are excluded from the credit to prevent duplicate reimbursement. Credit is not issued for unpaid amounts.

LOLA will confirm the credit amount and applicable terms in writing after processing the cancellation.

The credit may be applied toward one replacement event scheduled within twelve (12) months of the original event date. Credits will not be automatically expired or forfeited.

The replacement event is subject to LOLA's availability and then-current pricing.

Credit may be applied toward eligible experiences, packages, and add-ons, subject to the terms communicated when the credit is issued.

If the replacement booking costs more than the available credit, Client must pay the difference according to the applicable payment schedule.

If the replacement booking costs less, the unused amount remains rebooking credit and is not refunded in cash. Unused credit will not be automatically forfeited.

Credits are not automatically transferable to another person or organization unless approved by LOLA in writing.

### 8. Rescheduling

Client may request one (1) reschedule of a confirmed booking.

Rescheduling requests must be submitted at least **fourteen (14) calendar days before the original scheduled event date**, unless LOLA approves an exception in writing.

Requests may be submitted through:

- The Client Workspace
- Email to info@thelolabooth.com

All replacement dates and times are subject to availability.

LOLA is not required to cancel or modify another confirmed booking to accommodate a rescheduling request.

A rescheduling request does not guarantee approval or availability.

If Client's preferred replacement date is unavailable, Client may retain the original booking or select another available date.

An unavailable requested date does not count as the one permitted reschedule.

Only a successfully approved and confirmed date change counts toward the rescheduling allowance.

The original booking remains valid until a replacement date is confirmed in writing.

An approved reschedule may result in revised pricing, travel charges, staffing arrangements, or other costs, which must be disclosed and accepted before confirmation.

Payments already received will be applied toward the approved replacement booking, under this Agreement.

Any further rescheduling request requires written approval from LOLA.
