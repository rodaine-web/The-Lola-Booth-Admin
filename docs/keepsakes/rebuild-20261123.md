## Agreed access and fulfillment details

The booth has both branded and unbranded sources. Edited Digital Photo uses the
unbranded capture; Edited Digital Photo Strip uses the branded strip; the package
contains both. Never infer source provenance by trying to remove a flattened logo.

Phone retrieval is explicitly authorized for this phase without OTP: look up the
phone, collect email, record an unverified association and email secure photo links.
OTP verification is deferred to Twilio. Keep association provenance and access
audits so later verification can be added; never claim the phone/email is verified.

One approved postcard back; white background and protected logo/QR; only approved
accent elements recolored. Preserve the five note fonts and per-recipient notes.
Sender address is independent from purchaser shipping and recipient destinations.

Premium availability checks use documented WHCC/Gelato APIs and exact product,
variant, quantity, destination and artwork. Hold exceptions after payment rather
than silently substituting products. No supplier manufacturing merely on proposal
acceptance: enforce funding, artwork approval, materials and capacity requirements.
