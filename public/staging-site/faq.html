FAQ

Questions? We probably have an answer.

Everything you need to know before booking LOLA.

What is The LOLA Booth?

The LOLA Booth creates premium photo and video experiences for weddings, birthdays, showers, graduations, corporate events, brand activations, private celebrations, and more. Our current experiences include The Glam, The 360 Booth, The Vogue, and The Audio Guest Book. Good people. Better photos.

What experiences do you offer?

We currently offer four core experiences: The Glam A polished photo booth experience featuring flattering lighting, custom event design, instant digital sharing, and optional on-site photo printing. The 360 Booth An immersive video experience that captures guests from every angle with slow-motion effects, music, custom overlays, and instant sharing. The Vogue A life-size magazine-inspired photo experience designed to give guests their own editorial cover moment. The Audio Guest Book A vintage-inspired telephone experience that allows guests to leave heartfelt messages, funny stories, advice, and memories you can listen to after your event. You can also combine experiences for larger events or custom activations.

What types of events do you serve?

LOLA experiences work beautifully for: Weddings Birthday parties Bridal and baby showers Graduations Corporate events Galas Holiday parties Brand activations Product launches Festivals Private parties Anniversaries Reunions Community events If your event is not listed, contact us. We love creating custom experiences.

How much does a LOLA Booth experience cost?

Pricing depends on the experience, length of service, location, customization, printing, staffing, branding, and any additional enhancements. The Glam currently starts at $599. You can view available packages and starting prices on our Packages page. For corporate events, custom activations, multi-day events, or highly customized experiences, we will prepare a custom proposal.

What is included in The Glam Essential package?

The Glam Essential starts at $599 and includes: 2 hours of unlimited photo booth sessions Digital photos, GIFs, and Boomerangs Custom photo overlay Personalized welcome screen Instant text, email, and AirDrop sharing Premium backdrop selection Digital props Online event gallery Professional booth attendant Delivery, setup, and breakdown The Essential package is digital only. On-site printing is not included.

Do you offer photo printing?

Yes. On-site photo printing is included with select packages, including The Glam Signature and Luxe experiences. Printing can also be incorporated into certain custom experiences.

Can guests receive their photos immediately?

Yes. Depending on the selected experience and venue connectivity, guests can receive their photos or videos through options such as: Text message Email AirDrop QR code sharing Your event can also include an online gallery where available.

Can the booth be customized for my event?

Absolutely. Customization is one of the things we do best. Depending on your package, we can customize elements such as: Photo overlays Welcome screens Magazine cover designs Video overlays Backdrops Props Signage Booth graphics Brand colors Corporate logos Guest experiences Sharing screens For brands and corporate clients, we can create a fully customized activation.

Do you provide an online gallery?

Yes. Digital galleries are included with many of our photo and video booth packages. The gallery allows you to revisit and share moments captured during your event after the celebration is over.

Do you offer custom branding for corporate events?

Yes. The LOLA Booth can be adapted for product launches, corporate celebrations, sponsored events, experiential marketing, conferences, and brand activations. Custom options can include branded booth graphics, custom overlays, branded backdrops, event signage, custom props, guest-facing screens, QR experiences, digital galleries, and other activation elements. Contact us for a custom proposal.

Can I book more than one experience?

Yes. You can combine experiences such as: The Glam + The 360 Booth The Glam + Audio Guest Book The Vogue + Audio Guest Book Or create a larger custom package using multiple LOLA experiences. This is especially useful for weddings, corporate events, galas, festivals, and events with a larger guest count.

How long can I rent a booth?

Package lengths vary by experience. For example, The Glam currently offers options ranging from 2-hour service to up to 8 hours, as well as completely custom coverage. Additional booth hours may also be added when available.

Can I add more time to my package?

Yes, subject to availability. For The Glam, additional service time currently starts at $250 per hour. Rates for other experiences may vary. We recommend adding additional time before your event whenever possible so staffing and equipment availability can be guaranteed.

Can the booth be set up before my event begins?

Yes. Early setup and idle time can be arranged when needed. This is helpful when a venue requires vendors to complete setup several hours before guests arrive. Additional charges may apply depending on the event schedule.

How much space does a photo booth require?

Space requirements depend on the experience you select. The Glam generally requires less room than experiences such as The 360 Booth or The Vogue. Once we know your venue and selected experience, our team can provide recommended dimensions, electrical requirements, and placement guidance.

Does The 360 Booth need extra space?

Yes. Because guests stand on a platform while the camera moves around them, The 360 Booth requires a clear operating area around the platform. For safety and the best guest experience, we will confirm space requirements with you or your venue before the event.

Do you provide props?

Yes. Props are available with select packages and experiences. Options may include digital props, traditional event props, premium prop collections, or completely custom props designed for your celebration or brand activation.

Do you provide a booth attendant?

Yes. Our staffed booth experiences include a professional LOLA attendant unless specifically stated otherwise. The attendant handles setup, helps guests use the experience, keeps things moving, and manages the equipment throughout your event.

Does The Vogue require a large space?

The Vogue is a full-size magazine-style installation, so it requires more space than a traditional photo booth. We recommend discussing venue dimensions with us before booking so we can confirm that the installation will fit comfortably.

Can I choose my backdrop?

Yes. The Glam includes access to premium backdrop options depending on your package. Custom backdrops, branded backdrops, step-and-repeat designs, and specialty installations are also available upon request.

What is an Audio Guest Book?

The Audio Guest Book allows your guests to pick up a telephone and leave you a recorded message. Instead of only signing a traditional guest book, friends and family can leave: Congratulations Advice Funny stories Memories Personal messages Late-night messages that usually become everyone's favorites After the event, the recordings are delivered digitally.

Can I add the Audio Guest Book to another booth package?

Yes. The Audio Guest Book is available as a standalone experience or as an enhancement to another LOLA Booth experience. It currently starts at $299 when added to a booth package.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking as soon as your event date and venue are confirmed. Popular weekends, wedding season, holiday events, and corporate event dates can book well in advance. Last-minute bookings may be possible depending on equipment and staff availability.

How do I reserve my date?

Start by submitting an inquiry through our Book Now or availability form. Once we confirm availability and the details of your event, we will prepare the appropriate booking documents and payment information. A 30% down payment is required to secure your booking.

Is a deposit required?

Yes. A 30% down payment is required to secure your event date and reserve the equipment and team assigned to your booking. Your reservation is not considered confirmed until the required booking documents and payment have been completed.

What is your cancellation policy?

We require at least 48 hours' notice for cancellations. Any applicable refund, rescheduling, deposit, or cancellation terms will also be stated in your proposal, agreement, or booking documents.

Can I reschedule my event?

Contact us as soon as possible if your event date changes. We will do our best to move your reservation to the new date based on equipment and staff availability. Any changes to pricing, venue, service hours, or event requirements will be confirmed before the booking is updated.

Do you travel?

Yes. The LOLA Booth can accommodate events outside our immediate service area depending on availability and event requirements. Travel, delivery, parking, accommodations, or additional logistics may affect the final price. Send us your venue information and we will provide the appropriate quote.

Are delivery, setup, and breakdown included?

Delivery, setup, and breakdown are included in many LOLA packages. Additional charges may apply for events requiring extended travel, difficult venue access, unusual load-in requirements, early setup, idle time, or specialized installations.

What do you need from the venue?

Requirements vary by experience, but generally we need: Adequate booth space Access to a standard electrical outlet Safe load-in and load-out access A reasonably level setup area Reliable internet access when required for instant sharing We will provide specific requirements based on your selected experience.

Does the venue need Wi-Fi?

Internet access is helpful for features such as instant text and email sharing. Some sharing options may depend on venue connectivity. If venue Wi-Fi is unavailable or unreliable, let us know before your event so we can discuss available options.

Can The LOLA Booth be used outdoors?

Possibly. Outdoor events require additional planning because of weather, lighting, power, wind, ground conditions, and equipment protection. Covered and protected areas are generally preferred. Please tell us your event will be outdoors when requesting a quote.

Can you create a completely custom experience?

Yes. If one of our standard packages does not quite fit what you have in mind, we can build an experience around your event. Customizations can include: Multiple booths Custom service hours Custom booth builds 360 Video Booth Audio Guest Book Vogue installation On-site printing Custom backdrops Custom props Custom branding Corporate activations Additional attendants Early setup Idle time Multi-day rentals Tell us the idea and we will help build the experience.

Do you offer multi-day rentals?

Yes. Multi-day rentals and extended activations are available for corporate events, conferences, festivals, brand activations, and other special projects. Contact us for custom pricing.

Can we use our company logo on the photos and videos?

Yes. Corporate and brand clients can incorporate their logos and campaign artwork into appropriate parts of the experience, including photo templates, videos, sharing screens, signage, booth graphics, and other guest-facing assets.

Do we get to approve custom designs before the event?

For experiences involving custom creative work, we can provide artwork for review and approval before production or event deployment. This is particularly important for corporate branding, custom overlays, magazine artwork, signage, and other custom assets.

What happens if the equipment has a technical problem during the event?

Our staffed experiences include an attendant who monitors the booth and handles normal technical issues during service. We also maintain our equipment and event procedures to reduce interruptions and keep the experience running smoothly.

Are our photos private?

Event galleries and media are handled according to the selected package and event requirements. If your event requires additional privacy, restricted galleries, corporate confidentiality, or special media handling, please tell us during booking so those requirements can be addressed.

Can guests post their photos on social media?

Yes. LOLA experiences are designed to make sharing easy. Guests can download their content and share it to their preferred social platforms. Some custom brand activations may also include campaign hashtags, branded content, or other social engagement elements.
